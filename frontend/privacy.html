Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Known is a customer support and customer-memory application. This policy explains how Known handles information when you connect a business account or import customer data.

Information we handle

Known may handle customer information that a business intentionally imports or provides, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, order information, support conversations, and customer preferences or facts needed to provide support.

Google and Gmail data

When a business connects Gmail, Known requests only the Google account permissions needed for the Gmail support features that the business chooses to use. Known uses Gmail data to retrieve and process support messages, identify the relevant customer, provide customer-support context, and support responses and related product functionality.

Known does not sell Google user data. Known does not use Google user data for advertising. Known does not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the features the user has requested.

Storage and sharing

Customer and support data is stored and processed to operate Known. Data may be processed by service providers used to host the application, store application data, or provide requested AI functionality. Known does not sell customer data or Gmail data to third parties.

Security

Known uses access controls and authenticated application services intended to protect stored information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data choices

Businesses can disconnect integrations and manage customer data through Known. Requests concerning deletion or other data handling can be made through the support contact associated with the Known application.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Known's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about Known, contact the support email shown in the Known application or OAuth consent screen.
